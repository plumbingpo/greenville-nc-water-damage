Warning Signs for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Headaches, allergy flare-ups or breathing symptoms that ease whenever you leave the house
  • A flood or leak in the recent past that never got a professional dry-out
  • Dark staining or discoloration on walls, ceilings or around HVAC vents
  • Belongings in storage, including clothes and cardboard, showing spots or fuzz
  • Visible mold growth of any color on walls, ceilings, flooring or other surfaces
  • Warping, blistered paint or wallpaper lifting off the wall, all clues to moisture you cannot see
  • Mold or mildew that comes back soon after you clean it

Mold Remediation Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • In bathrooms, basements and other damp areas, choose paint and drywall rated mold-resistant
  • Clean and maintain your HVAC system and change filters regularly to prevent mold in ductwork
  • Check that exhaust fans in the kitchen, laundry room and bathrooms move enough air
  • When windows, piping or other cold surfaces start to sweat, correct it without waiting
  • Hang or launder wet fabrics promptly and wipe up spills as they happen
  • Overflowing gutters send water into basements and crawl spaces, so clean them on a schedule

Why Hire a Professional for Mold Remediation?

Mold remediation is not a DIY project. When a colony is disturbed and nothing contains it, millions of spores go into the air, where they may settle in clean rooms and expose your family more than the mold did before. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

Just as important, we deal with moisture, the underlying cause of every mold problem. If the moisture is left alone, taking away what you can see only buys time, and regrowth often shows up within weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.
